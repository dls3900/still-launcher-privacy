Still Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Still Launcher is an Android home screen app developed by Aditya Gupta. This policy explains how the app accesses and handles information. Privacy inquiries: adityagupta19092004@gmail.com.

Information accessed on your device

Still Launcher reads the launchable apps made available by Android, including app names, icons, activity identifiers, and associated device profiles. It uses this information to display, search, and open your apps. Profile availability is controlled by Android. This information is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer.

The app displays the current date and time using your device settings and displays your wallpaper through Android. It does not read your wallpaper file. Search text is used locally to filter apps and is not saved as search history.

Information stored locally

In the app's private preferences, Still Launcher saves your favorite app identifiers and their order, a short list of apps recently opened through Still Launcher, display settings (content position, alphabet side, and wallpaper dimming), and whether the default-home prompt has been shown. Recent apps are based only on launches from Still Launcher; the app does not read system-wide usage history.

These preferences support the launcher features. App names and icons are also cached in memory while the app is running.

Collection, sharing, and permissions

Still Launcher does not transmit your app list, preferences, recent launches, or search text to Aditya Gupta or to advertising or analytics services. It does not sell this information. The app has no internet permission, advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, user accounts, or developer-operated server.

It does not request access to your contacts, messages, location, microphone, camera, or personal files. Opening another app or its Android settings is a user-initiated action; the other app handles its own information under its own privacy policy.

Android backup and device transfers

Android backup is enabled for this app. Depending on your device settings and backup provider, Android may include the app's saved preferences in a backup or device transfer and may restore them later. The developer does not receive or control these backups. Your device or backup provider's policies govern this process. Manage backup and existing backups through your device or provider settings.

Security, retention, and deletion

Saved preferences are held in app-private storage protected by Android's app isolation. Still Launcher retains them until you change them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Recent-launch identifiers are automatically replaced as new apps are opened through the launcher. Memory caches are temporary and are released when the app process ends.

You can remove or reset favorites in launcher settings. To delete all locally saved launcher data, use Android Settings > Apps > Still Launcher > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies by device), or uninstall Still Launcher. Clearing local storage or uninstalling does not necessarily delete an existing system backup; manage that separately with your backup provider. No developer-held account or server data exists to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information handling changes, this policy will be updated and its revision date changed. The policy is available in Still Launcher settings under Privacy Policy and on the public policy page linked in the Google Play listing.
